It helps them find jobs, apply, and keep their resume ready in one simple place.

T.E.T

T.E.T

T.E.T is an e-commerce website I built on my own with WordPress, selling Japanese products to French customers.
I started from zero, with no knowledge of tech or WordPress, so I learned by following YouTube tutorials before choosing a theme.
Once I chose the theme, I replaced all of its content myself, using photos I took.
I taught myself GIMP to edit the photos, fixing the lighting and anything else that didn't fit the website.
I also set up card payments with Stripe and tested them to make sure checkout worked.
Built with: WordPress, Stripe, GIMP.
